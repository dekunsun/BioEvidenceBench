Family—TURDIDÆ.

Subfamily—SYLVIINÆ.

PALLAS' WILLOW-WARBLER.

Phylloscpous proregulus.

Asingle example of this species was obtained in Norfolk, in October, 1896.

A good many Chiﬀchaﬀs visited the Shetlands between April 15th and 24th,
1904; in May of the same year one was seen by Mr. John S. Tluloch at
Lerwick, and on May 27th, one was killed there by a cat.
