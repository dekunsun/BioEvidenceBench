- `empty_text`: plates and blank pages. Their `text` is `""`; they are excluded from CER and sampling.
- `text_pages_with_reading_order`: among pages with text, share whose PAGE XML has an explicit `<ReadingOrder>`. Pages without one fall back to document order, which can scramble text (dataset card).
- `pages_with_illegible`: pages containing U+FFFD, which the transcribers used for *illegible glyph*.
